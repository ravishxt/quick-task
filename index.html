Quick Task for Asana

Turn selected text into an Asana task.

Quick Task for Asana is a Chrome extension. Select text on any page and press one shortcut. The text and the page link go to Asana, and you stay on your page.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

How it works

  1. Select textUse any page in Chrome, such as an email or a document.
  2. Press the shortcutOption+Shift+A on Mac, or Alt+Shift+A on Windows.
  3. Press EnterThe task goes to your Asana project with the page link.

What you get

Delivery
Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store. After you pay, Dodo Payments sends a license key to your email. Paste it into the extension settings to unlock unlimited tasks.
What you need
Chrome 116 or later, an Asana account, and an Asana personal access token. The settings page shows where to add your token.
Privacy
The extension reads a page only when you press the shortcut or click its icon. It has no analytics and no developer server. Privacy policy.
Limit
Some apps, such as Google Docs, draw text in a way the extension cannot read. In these apps, the page title becomes the task name.
