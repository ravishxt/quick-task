What the extension reads

When you press the shortcut, click the icon, or use the right-click menu, it reads the selected text, title, and link of the current tab. If you right-click an image, it reads that image, or a screenshot of the visible part of it. It does not read pages at any other time.

What it stores in your browser

Your Asana token, your license key, your last workspace, project, section, and assignee, your project, section, people, and tag lists, including profile photo links, any unsent draft, and your monthly task count. The task count may sync through your Chrome account. The token does not.

What it sends

Task text, any image you choose to attach, and your token go to Asana to create the task. Your license key goes to Dodo Payments to check your plan. Nothing else is sent.

What it does not do

There are no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no developer server. The developer never receives your token or tasks. Your data is not sold or shared.

Payments

Dodo Payments handles checkout and collects your payment and billing details. The developer can see order details in the Dodo Payments dashboard, such as your email address, but never your full card number.

This website

This website has no analytics and sets no cookies. GitHub Pages hosts it and may log visitor IP addresses for security.

Removing data

Uninstall the extension to delete its local data. Revoke the token in the Asana developer console.
