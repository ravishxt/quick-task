Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Refunds

You can get a full refund of your first payment if you ask within 7 days of that payment. To ask, email ravishxt@gmail.com from the address you used at checkout.

Monthly renewals are not refunded, except where the law requires a refund.

The refund goes back to your original payment method. Your bank sets how long it takes to appear. A refund ends your paid plan.

Cancellation

You can cancel at any time. There are two ways:

Sign in to the Dodo Payments customer portal with your checkout email, open your subscription, and select "Cancel subscription".

Or email ravishxt@gmail.com. We reply within 2 business days.

After you cancel

If you cancel at the next billing date, you keep unlimited tasks until then. After your subscription ends, your license key stops working and the extension returns to the free plan of 10 tasks a month.
